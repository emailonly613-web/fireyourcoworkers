# Visual Contract

Authoritative visual direction:
`docs/APPROVED-VISUAL-DIRECTION-2026-07-29.png`.

## Preserve from the approved direction

- A dominant glass-and-metal elevator.
- Cinematic navy office depth with warm amber light.
- Expressive illustrated coworkers whose silhouettes communicate their shapes.
- Chunky tactile controls, integrated HR escalation, paper motion, squish, and a
  visually immediate comedy payoff.
- Clear green valid, red violation, and amber action states.

## Remove or replace

- Every public use of the obsolete internal codename or protected puzzle-game mark.
- Phone-frame collage composition as a literal website layout.
- Fake views, likes, comments, revenue, testimonials, or engagement counts.
- Emoji, feature-card dashboards, colored blocks, and generic SaaS presentation.

## Checkpoint 2 gate

- The first viewport must contain the Fire Your Coworkers wordmark, living elevator,
  Sleeping Intern, Micro-Managing CEO, Broken Copy Machine, animated paper, integrated
  HR meter, muted sound control, and one obvious action.
- Desktop and mobile must feel intentionally composed, not merely resized.
- The elevator remains the largest visual object.
- One sentence maximum appears before interaction.
