# 4. NON-NEGOTIABLE QUALITY BAR

## Visual rejection conditions

Reject the build if any of these are true:

- The first viewport resembles a SaaS dashboard.
- The first viewport is mostly text or cards.
- The game is the only thing visible at the root URL.
- Characters are emojis, generic rectangles, or stock icons.
- The playable level visually downgrades from the hero.
- The elevator is small or secondary.
- No movement is visible before the player acts.
- The joke requires a paragraph to understand.
- Mobile feels like a shrunken desktop page.
- The screenshots are not materially better than the rejected Claude screenshots.

## Engineering rejection conditions

Reject the build if:

- It only compiles locally.
- No public URL exists despite available deployment access.
- Drag fails on touch.
- Page scrolling disrupts placement.
- Invalid placement corrupts state.
- Undo or restart is unreliable.
- PWA update/cache behavior traps stale code.
- The root page cannot reload directly.
- Console errors appear in the normal flow.
- Required screenshots or recordings are missing.
